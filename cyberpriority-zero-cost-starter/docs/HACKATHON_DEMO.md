# 3-minute demo

1. Open the dashboard and point out the prioritized incident queue.
2. Select the top incident and show the score, factors, decision trace and correlated events.
3. Click **SIMULATE ATTACK**.
4. Show the new synthetic attack entering the queue.
5. Explain that several related events are grouped into one contextual incident.
6. Show the risk score and why the incident became P1.
7. Use a simulated response button. Explain that the hackathon build does not touch external systems.
8. Finish with: "The goal is not to generate more alerts; it is to help analysts understand which alerts deserve attention first and why."
